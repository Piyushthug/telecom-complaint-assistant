# Internet Outage Complaint Policy

**Document Type:** Customer Complaint Policy  
**Document ID:** KB-NET-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Applies To:** Residential Internet Customers

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standard process for handling customer complaints related to complete internet outages.

The objective is to provide customers with a consistent response, identify the appropriate troubleshooting path, and determine when a complaint should be escalated.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies when a customer reports that their internet service is completely unavailable.

Examples include:

- Internet connection is completely down.
- Customer cannot access any websites or applications.
- Customer reports that the router has no internet connectivity.
- Customer reports repeated loss of internet service.

This policy does not specifically cover slow internet performance. Slow-speed complaints should follow the Slow Internet Troubleshooting SOP.

---

## 3. Required Complaint Information

The support system should capture the following information when available:

- Customer ID
- Complaint ID
- Service type
- Approximate outage start time
- Customer description of the problem
- Whether the customer has already attempted troubleshooting
- Number of previous contacts for the same issue

If information is unavailable, the support agent should not invent or assume the missing information.

---

## 4. Initial Troubleshooting

For a new internet outage complaint, the support process should recommend the following basic checks:

1. Confirm that the customer's router or gateway is powered on.
2. Check whether the device shows an internet connection.
3. Ask the customer to verify the physical network connections.
4. Restart the router or gateway if appropriate.
5. Allow sufficient time for the device to reconnect.
6. Check whether the internet connection has been restored.

Troubleshooting instructions should only be provided when they are relevant to the reported problem.

---

## 5. Repeated Complaints

If the customer has contacted support multiple times for the same unresolved outage, the complaint should receive additional attention.

Examples of repeated-contact indicators include:

- Multiple complaints for the same outage.
- Multiple support contacts without resolution.
- Customer reports that previously recommended troubleshooting did not resolve the issue.

Repeated contact alone does not guarantee a particular resolution or compensation. The complaint should be evaluated according to the applicable escalation policy.

---

## 6. Escalation

An internet outage complaint may be considered for escalation when:

- The service remains unavailable after the recommended troubleshooting steps.
- The customer has contacted support repeatedly for the same unresolved problem.
- The issue requires investigation by a specialized support team.
- The complaint meets the conditions defined in the Escalation Policy.

The support assistant should not claim that an escalation has been completed unless an actual escalation action has been performed by the support system.

---

## 7. Response Guidelines

Responses should:

- Acknowledge the customer's reported problem.
- Be clear and professional.
- Avoid blaming the customer.
- Provide only troubleshooting steps relevant to the complaint.
- Avoid unsupported promises.
- Avoid inventing outage causes.
- Avoid guaranteeing a resolution time unless such information is available from an authorized system.

For customers expressing strong frustration, the response should acknowledge the inconvenience before providing troubleshooting or escalation information.

---

## 8. Prohibited Responses

The assistant must not:

- Invent a network outage.
- Claim that a technician has been dispatched when this has not been confirmed.
- Promise a specific restoration time without authorized information.
- Claim that a refund or compensation has been approved without confirmation.
- State that an issue is resolved when resolution has not been verified.
- Provide troubleshooting instructions unrelated to the customer's reported issue.

---

## 9. Example Complaint

**Customer Complaint:**

"My internet has been down since yesterday. I already contacted support twice but nobody fixed it."

**Relevant Classification:**

- Category: INTERNET_OUTAGE
- Contact Pattern: REPEATED_CONTACT
- Sentiment: Potentially Negative

**Recommended Handling:**

Acknowledge the outage and previous support contacts, determine whether basic troubleshooting has already been attempted, and evaluate the complaint against the escalation policy.
