# Customer Refund Policy

**Document Type:** Customer Support Policy  
**Document ID:** KB-BILL-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the general process for handling customer requests for refunds related to billing complaints.

## 2. Refund Requests

Customers may request a refund when they believe they were incorrectly charged.

Common scenarios include:

- Duplicate billing
- Incorrect service charge
- Incorrect plan charge
- Billing adjustment approved by an authorized team

A refund request does not automatically mean that a refund has been approved.

## 3. Verification

Before communicating a refund decision, the support process should verify:

- Customer account
- Relevant billing transaction
- Amount in question
- Reason for the refund request
- Applicable billing records

Only authorized systems or personnel can confirm whether a refund has been approved.

## 4. Response Rules

The assistant may explain that a refund request can be reviewed.

The assistant must not:

- Guarantee a refund.
- Claim that a refund has been processed without confirmation.
- Invent refund amounts.
- Invent transaction details.
- Promise a specific refund processing time unless authorized information is available.

## 5. Escalation

Escalation may be appropriate when:

- Billing information cannot be verified.
- The requested refund requires manual approval.
- The customer disputes the result of a previous investigation.
- The issue falls outside the standard refund process.

## 6. Example

**Customer:**  
"I was charged twice. Give me my money back."

**Recommended handling:**

Acknowledge the concern, explain that the billing transaction needs to be verified, and avoid guaranteeing a refund before the billing information has been reviewed.
