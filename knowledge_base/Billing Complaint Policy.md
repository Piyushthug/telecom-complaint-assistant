# Billing Complaint Policy

**Document Type:** Customer Complaint Policy  
**Document ID:** KB-BILL-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Applies To:** Residential Customers

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standard process for handling customer complaints related to billing, unexpected charges, duplicate charges, and incorrect amounts.

## 2. Complaint Types

Common billing complaints include:

- Unexpected charge
- Duplicate charge
- Incorrect monthly bill
- Incorrect plan charge
- Charge for a service the customer does not recognize
- Billing amount different from the expected amount

## 3. Required Information

The support process should capture, when available:

- Customer ID
- Complaint ID
- Billing account
- Invoice or billing period
- Amount in dispute
- Customer explanation of the issue
- Previous billing complaints

The assistant must not invent invoice numbers, amounts, or transaction details.

## 4. Initial Investigation

The support process should:

1. Identify the billing period involved.
2. Review the customer's stated concern.
3. Compare the disputed amount with available billing information.
4. Determine whether the complaint may relate to a plan, service, adjustment, or duplicate charge.
5. Refer the issue for additional investigation when the available information is insufficient.

## 5. Duplicate Charges

When a customer reports being charged twice:

- Confirm the billing information using an authorized billing system.
- Do not immediately state that a refund is approved.
- If duplicate charging is confirmed, follow the applicable refund process.

## 6. Unrecognized Charges

For an unrecognized charge:

- Do not assume the charge is fraudulent.
- Request or retrieve the necessary billing information.
- Follow the appropriate investigation procedure.

## 7. Response Guidelines

The assistant should:

- Acknowledge the billing concern.
- Clearly explain the next step.
- Avoid confirming an incorrect charge without evidence.
- Avoid promising a refund before approval.
- Avoid inventing billing information.

## 8. Example

**Customer:**  
"I was charged twice for my internet service this month."

**Classification:**  
`BILLING`

**Recommended handling:**  
Review the relevant billing information and determine whether a duplicate charge exists. If confirmed, follow the Refund Policy.
